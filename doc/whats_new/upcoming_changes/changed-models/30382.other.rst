- :class:`mixture.GaussianMixture` and :class:`mixture.BayesianGaussianMixture`
  with ``covariance_type="diag"`` or ``"spherical"`` can produce different
  ``covariances_`` (and therefore different subsequent EM iterates) for
  components whose variance is small relative to the second moment of the data,
  most noticeably for ``float32`` inputs. Other components are unchanged.
  By :user:`Olivier Grisel <ogrisel>`.
