- :class:`mixture.GaussianMixture` and :class:`mixture.BayesianGaussianMixture`
  with ``covariance_type="diag"`` or ``"spherical"`` can produce different
  ``covariances_`` (and therefore different subsequent EM iterates), most
  noticeably for ``float32`` inputs whose features have a large magnitude.
  ``float64`` results change only by rounding.
  By :user:`Olivier Grisel <ogrisel>`.
